Generated by `plot_storm_panel.py --tag m65` (FINDINGS §72). The planting-season test (`storm_panel_m65.md`) used
the 7 spring storm years only. Here: every modelled storm tide of July to February, grouped by the first CDL
year whose planting can respond (2012: storm2011aug; 2013: sandy2012+storm2012dec; 2016: storm2016jan; 2019: storm2018oct+storm2018sep; 2021: storm2020aug; 2022: storm2021oct; 2024: storm2024jan). Flooded = a storm of that off-season floods the field (tide only,
>= 10% > 5 cm); comparison = flood-prone fields it missed ("prone-dry") or fields no storm tide floods ("never"),
weighted to the same heights; each field against its own quiet years (2008, 2009, 2015, 2017, 2018, 2020, 2025). k = 0 the first
season after the storm, k = 1 the one after. Percentage points of field area (shares) or of the chance (yes/no), with
95% intervals from resampling fields. 2012, 2022, 2024 also hold a planting-season storm, so "all without"
leaves them out. Yes/no outcomes from 2024 on are blank (CDL's product change), and 2023 for the two
left outcomes. Per-storm-year results vs prone-dry excluding zero: 15 of 78 (about 3.9 by chance).
