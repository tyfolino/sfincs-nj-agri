# Field farming histories from Landsat, 1984-2025 (2026-10-05)

Figure: `figs/field_history.png`.

Generated by `scripts/field_history.py` (method in its docstring). Units = NJDEP LU/LC farm polygons (`scripts/build_field_units.py`): all farmed-in-2012 fields below MHHW + 3 m, 2500 of 9332 higher ones (weighted ×3.7), every 1995-2012 conversion, and 200 stable-marsh references.
