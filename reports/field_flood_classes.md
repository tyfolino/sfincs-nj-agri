Generated by `scripts/field_flood_classes.py` (method in its docstring; rules in `agri_sfincs/fieldflood.py`). Map: `figs/field_flood_classes{,_zoom}.png`. Per-field table: `data/analysis/field_flood_classes.csv`.

Fields: 13,699 NJDEP LU/LC 2020 farm polygons, 76,419 ha. Runs used: intact_slr000, intact_slr030, intact_slr060, intact_slr100, removed_slr000, removed_slr030, removed_slr060, removed_slr100, breach_all_farm_slr000, breach_all_farm_slr030, breach_all_farm_slr060, breach_all_farm_slr100.

A field floods in a run when > 10% of it sees > 5 cm at least once in the month. 'Overtopped' = floods even with the dikes and lies behind an NLD levee; 'exposed' = the same with no levee. 'Dike-dependent +L' = floods at +L only without the dikes.
